\begin{cnabstract}
本模板用于演示博士学位论文的基本组织方式与常见排版元素。文档包含中英文封面、创新点、中英文摘要、目录、图表索引、正文、参考文献、附录以及科研成果目录。使用时应将示例内容逐项替换为自己的研究内容，并依据所在学院当年的要求核对格式。

模板通过独立配置文件管理论文信息，提供公式、三线表、组合图、跨页表格与文献引用示例。正文采用自然分页与适度浮动方式组织图文，关键词紧跟摘要正文。示例数据仅用于展示排版方式，不代表任何真实实验结果。

\cnkeywords{博士学位论文；LaTeX；图文排版；开源模板}
\end{cnabstract}

\begin{enabstract}
This template demonstrates the structure and common typesetting elements of a doctoral dissertation. It includes Chinese and English covers, a statement of contributions, abstracts, a table of contents, lists of figures and tables, chapters, references, an appendix, and a list of publications. Replace the examples with your own work and verify the current requirements of your department.

The template stores dissertation metadata in a separate file and provides examples of equations, tables, subfigures, multipage tables, and citations. Flexible placement allows text and figures to share pages. Keywords follow the abstract body. All example measurements are illustrative and do not represent research findings.

\enkeywords{Doctoral dissertation; LaTeX; Typesetting; Open-source template}
\end{enabstract}
